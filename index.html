01 · The campus

One wedge of land, three roads around it.

The campus sits on a tight wedge at Kashmere Gate. Madarsa Road wraps it on two sides, Lothian Road runs along the third, and Ambedkar University is the neighbour on the Kela Ghat Marg side.

02 · Central lawn

Everything faces the lawn.

The central lawn is the middle of the map. The auditorium and the national flag stand on its west edge, and the departments, hostels and offices wrap around the other sides on one loop road.

03 · The hostels

Kaveri and Krishna.

The two girls' hostels line the Madarsa Road side, each built around an open-to-sky courtyard. The tuck shop, the creche and the start-up office sit close by.

04 · North blocks

Mechanical, AI and the admin block.

Along the top of the lawn: the CoE in Artificial Intelligence, AI and Data Science, Mechanical and Automation Engineering, and the Administration Block, which also houses Architecture and Planning.

05 · South blocks

ECE, IT and the long social science block.

On the Lothian Road side: Electronics and Communication, Information Technology, Applied Sciences and Humanities, Management, the Social Science Block, the Anveshan Foundation and Rekhi CoE, and the NCC block.

06 · East row

Library row.

Small blocks on the lawn's east edge: the Academic Branch, the Digital Library, the Computer Lab, the Examination Branch and the canteen.

07 · Getting here

Kashmere Gate Metro, right next door.

Kashmere Gate is an interchange of three metro lines: the elevated Red Line and the underground Yellow and Violet lines. Gate 7 is the closest to campus, about 340 m in a straight line from the IGDTUW gate. The ISBT, Old Delhi Junction and the GPO are close by too.
